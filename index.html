An AI assistant for frontline workers

Rocket Learning, Product Manager
2025 to now

25K+Anganwadi Workers using Shiksha Saathi

The problem

Anganwadi Workers run India's early-childhood centres with little support and patchy connectivity. They already live on WhatsApp, so that is where help had to be.

What I did

Defined what to build and built parts of it myself, including the WhatsApp flows on Glific and the LLM evals, in partnership with OpenAI. Built personas and behavioural segments from survey and usage data across 220K users, and ran behavioural interventions with Stanford for 5K+ workers.

What changed

Shiksha Saathi was launched by three state governments, including by the Chief Minister of Maharashtra at AI Summit 2026.

Setting up how a product team ships

Revlitix, Product Manager
2023

15+integrations shipped, each with a full spec

The problem

A marketing analytics platform with a capable engineering and design team, but no product manager and no shared rhythm for delivery.

What I did

Took charge of the roadmap and set up sprints and design-to-engineering handoffs for 20+ people. Wrote a spec for every integration covering APIs, costs, data to pull, reports and sample queries, so engineers could start without research.

What changed

Shipped SSO, role-based access control, audit logs, funnel reporting and workflow automation alongside the integrations. The team kept the delivery practices after I left.

From employee #3 to enterprise access controls

Airmeet, Growth Lead then Product Manager
2020 to 2023

3 → 500+people, as Airmeet grew around virtual events

The problem

Virtual events before COVID were a market that had to be created, and later, enterprise customers needed to control who could access sensitive event data.

What I did

Built growth from the first customers to a 50+ member team handling hundreds of leads daily, with a voice-of-customer system feeding product and engineering. Then moved into product, leading a 12-person cross-functional team that owned enterprise role-based access controls and SSO.

What changed

Enterprise customers could manage sensitive account access themselves, and customer insight reached the teams building the product instead of getting lost in sales and support.

Keeping quality consistent across rural centres

Krishworks, Co-Founder
2017 to 2019

2,000+children across 15+ centres in rural West Bengal

The problem

Our tech-enabled English learning centres were spread across rural West Bengal. A technology-heavy monitoring system would have burdened centre managers and still missed what happened in class.

What I did

Built a human operating system instead: regular conversations with centre managers, required submissions and worksheet reviews, combined with app usage data. Drove three pivots through user research, including from maths to English and to a teacher-centric model.

What changed

It became the team's main view into how centres were doing. Krishworks was backed by IIM Calcutta and Zinnov.
